01

Who we are

AI calc is developed and published by Ajirix Technologies ("we", "us"). We are responsible for the information described in this policy. You can reach us at any time using the contact details at the end of this page.

02

The app works offline

All calculations happen on your device. The expressions you type, your results, your calculation history, the functions you define and your settings are stored only on your phone. We never receive them.
03

Information we collect

When the app has an internet connection, it quietly checks in with our server (at most once every 15 minutes while you use it) to find out about updates and notices. Each check-in shares only the following:

Anonymous device IDA random-looking code

Recognises the same phone between check-ins. It is a one-way scrambled form of Android's app-specific ID, so it cannot be traced back to you or used by other apps.

Phone detailse.g. Samsung SM-A125F

Manufacturer, brand and model, so we can support devices and target fixes.

Android versione.g. Android 12

Tells us which fixes apply to your phone.

App versione.g. 1.0

Lets us tell you when a newer version is available.

IP addresse.g. 102.89.x.x

Received automatically with every internet connection. We keep the first and latest one for security and abuse prevention.

Usage datesFirst and last check-in

Dates and a count of check-ins, to understand how many phones use each version.

Message deliverye.g. notice #4 delivered

Which update notices or messages your phone has received.

We do not collect your name, phone number, email, contacts, photos, precise location, or anything you type into the calculator. The app requests no permissions other than internet access.

04

How we use it

  • To tell you about new versions and, when a version has a serious problem, to require an update.
  • To show important notices, either to everyone or to a specific phone (for example, a reply to a support request).
  • To understand which phones and app versions are in use, so we can test and fix them.
  • To keep the service secure and prevent abuse.

We rely on our legitimate interest in keeping the app up to date, working and secure. We do not use this information for advertising or profiling, and we never sell it.

05

Who we share it with

The information is stored with Supabase, the cloud database provider we use to run the check-in service. Supabase processes it only on our behalf. Its servers may be located outside your country.

We do not share it with anyone else, except where the law requires us to.

06

How long we keep it

We keep each phone's record only for as long as it is needed for the purposes above. You can ask us to delete your record at any time. If you uninstall the app, it stops sending any information.

07

Your rights

Depending on where you live, including under the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023, you have the right to:

  • ask what information we hold about your phone and get a copy of it;
  • ask us to correct or delete it;
  • object to, or ask us to limit, how we use it;
  • complain to your data protection authority. In Nigeria, this is the Nigeria Data Protection Commission (NDPC).

To make a request, contact us and tell us your phone model and roughly when you installed AI calc, so we can find the right record. We will respond within 30 days.

08

Security and children

Information travels over encrypted (HTTPS) connections, and our database is locked so the app itself cannot read it back. Only Ajirix Technologies can access it.

AI calc is a general-purpose calculator suitable for students. It does not ask for or knowingly collect any personal details from anyone, including children.

09

Other services and changes

The developer page in the app links to our Facebook, WhatsApp and Instagram profiles. If you open them, those services' own privacy policies apply.

If we change this policy, we will update the effective date at the top. For important changes, we will also tell you inside the app.
